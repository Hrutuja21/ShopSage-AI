4. BUSINESS RECOMMENDATIONS
----------------------------------------
1. Prioritize high-risk orders for manual review before promised delivery dates.
2. Monitor regional performance using both order volume and observed late-delivery rates.
3. Investigate states with large gaps between predicted and actual late-delivery rates.
4. Review monthly trends to identify changes in delivery performance and potential distribution shift.
5. Track false positives and false negatives separately. False positives create unnecessary interventions, while false negatives represent late orders the model misses.
6. Validate the model on newer data before deploying automated customer notifications or operational decisions.
